Privacy Policy

AppSweep — Update Checker (Mac App Store). Last updated: October 2026.

Support

Summary

AppSweep doesn't collect, store or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What happens on your Mac

  • AppSweep reads the names, versions and bundle identifiers of apps in the folder you choose. This information stays on your Mac.
  • Your settings and the list of ignored apps are stored locally.

Network requests

  • To check for updates, AppSweep contacts the update sources of your apps directly (for example a developer's Sparkle feed, the GitHub API or Apple's App Store lookup). These requests contain the app being checked, but no personal information.
  • All requests use HTTPS. We don't run servers that receive your data.
